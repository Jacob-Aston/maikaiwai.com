Pulu

Compost

ʻO ka pulu e hoʻomomona ana i ka lepo me nā ʻōpala mālama ʻāina.
Compost enriches the soil using earth-caring scraps and waste.
E hana ʻia ka pulu ma ka home me nā lau a me nā koʻiʻula i kēlā me kēia pule.
You make compost at home with leaves and kitchen scraps every week.
ʻO ka pulu ka mea e ulu ikaika ai kāu māla lanakila i nā makahiki.
Compost is what makes your victory garden grow strong for years to come.

Moa

Chickens

We build a small, proper chicken coop to care for the chickens right here in Honolulu.
Nā moa e kōkua i kāu māla lanakila me ka hoʻomaʻemaʻe ʻana a me nā hua ʻono.
Chickens help your victory garden with natural pest control and delicious eggs.

Irrigation

Smart irrigation channels save water for your victory garden.

Hunter-certified systems deliver the exact right amount of water to every plant.
E ulu maikaʻi nā mea kanu me ka wai ma ka ʻauwai hou a me ka ʻenehana hou.
Plants thrive with efficient water from the new irrigation and modern tech.
